# Services

All information is fictional and belongs to the AgentFlow demonstration.

The company reviews technical requirements, prepares nonbinding quotations, and manufactures components after a separately agreed order.

A quote request should include the product, quantity, target delivery date, dimensions, hole patterns, material requirements, and any finish requirements.

The public AgentFlow demo does not transmit requests, upload drawings, contact staff, or place orders. Its quote workflow only illustrates how a request could be structured.
