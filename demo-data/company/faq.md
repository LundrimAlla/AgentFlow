# Frequently asked questions

All information is fictional and belongs to the AgentFlow demonstration.

Who does the company serve? Alpine Components serves equipment builders and small manufacturing teams buying components for business use.

Can I obtain an immediate price? No. A price requires specifications and technical review; there is no published price list.

Can I request a particular delivery date? A target delivery date may be included in a request, but it is not a confirmed delivery date.

Can I use this demo to order? No. Demo scenarios do not submit requests or create orders.
